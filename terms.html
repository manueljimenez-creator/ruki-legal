2. What Ruki is

An app that generates and adjusts running training plans from your running data, with a coach built on artificial intelligence that you can talk to, and a social side to connect with other runners and with running clubs.

3. Who you are talking to: the coach is an AI

The Ruki coach is an artificial intelligence system, not a person. You are answered by a language model that receives your training data as context, and this is stated on the chat screen itself.

Where what it tells you comes from. The plan is calculated by our own algorithm from your real running history (volume, paces, heart rate and load over recent weeks), using widely accepted endurance training principles — gradual progression, acute/chronic workload ratio, deload weeks, tapering — collected in our own corpus of training texts. Its limits: it does not know your medical history, it has not watched you run, it does not assess your technique or your biomechanics, and it can be wrong. What it is for: organising your training. Nothing else.

4. Ruki is not a doctor

This is the most important thing on this page.

Ruki does not give medical advice, does not diagnose and does not treat any injury or illness. The plans and recommendations are general sporting information, generated automatically from your data, and they do not replace the judgement of a doctor, a physiotherapist or a personal trainer.

  • Before starting a plan, and especially if you have or have had heart, respiratory, joint or muscular problems, if you are pregnant, if you are recovering from an injury or if you have not exercised for a long time, consult a healthcare professional.
  • The niggles you declare in the app help adjust the plan, but they are not a diagnosis and do not guarantee that the plan is safe for you.
  • Stop training and seek medical help if you feel chest pain, dizziness, excessive breathlessness or any pain that gets worse as you run.
  • You train at your own risk. You decide which session you do and at what intensity; the app proposes, it does not compel.
