Support

Ruki is a running training app with a coach that talks to you in a chat. If something isn't working, or you want your data deleted, it's all here.

Write to us

manolojc02@gmail.com

We reply within 2-3 working days. Tell us what you expected to happen and what happened instead, and send a screenshot if you can: that solves most things on the first reply.

If it's about your training — a session that doesn't add up, a pace that looks wrong, a week you want moved — tell the coach in the app's chat. That's what it's there for, and it answers straight away.

Your Strava connection

What we import. When you connect Strava we bring in your activities: date, sport type, name, distance, moving time, pace, average and max heart rate, cadence, elevation gain, per-kilometre splits and laps. The first time we import about twelve months of history; after that, each new activity arrives on its own.

What we do NOT import. The route. Strava sends us the map of your route and your start and end points, and we discard all of it before storing anything: we don't know where you run or where you set off from.

Who sees it. Only you. Your public profile — the one other Ruki users see — shows your name, city, bio, goal, club and the personal bests you type in yourself. Never distance, pace, heart rate, or any summary or chart derived from them.

How to disconnect. Settings → Connections → Disconnect Strava. We stop importing right then. You can also revoke access from your Strava settings.

Deleting your account

Settings → Danger zone → Delete account. Everything goes: your profile, your plans, your conversations with the coach and the imported activities. It's immediate and it can't be undone.

What Ruki is not

Ruki is not a doctor and does not replace one. If you have an injury, pain that won't go away, or any health concern, see a professional before following a plan.
