Ruki Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

Ruki is a running training app with a coach built on artificial intelligence. To work, it needs to know how you run, and that is personal data — some of it health data. This policy explains which data, what for, who it is shared with and how you delete it.

  • Controller: Manuel Jiménez Carmona, NIF 70422792N, self-employed (Spain)
  • Address: Calle Monte Perdido 7A, 28760 Tres Cantos, Madrid, Spain
  • Contact: manolojc02@gmail.com

For anything to do with your data, write to that address.

2. What data we process

What you give us when you sign up

First and last name, username, email address, date of birth, sex, city, profile photo, and optionally your bio and your Instagram handle.

What you tell us about your training

Your experience level, your goal (race, distance, date and target time), how many days a week you want to train, your schedule and commitments, and any niggles or injuries you declare, along with any note you write about them.

What we import from your connected services

If you connect Strava, we import your activities: date and time, sport type, activity name, distance, time, pace, average and maximum heart rate, cadence, elevation, per-kilometre splits and laps.

What the app itself generates

Your training plan and its sessions, your fitness metrics (training load, reference paces, race predictions), your conversations with your coach and the summaries it produces from them.

The social side

Your friendships and requests, the clubs you belong to, the meet-ups you sign up for, your notifications and the users you have blocked.

Moderation data

If you report content, we keep your report, the reason, whatever you tell us and a copy of the reported content exactly as it was at that moment (otherwise the author edits it and there is nothing left to review). If someone reports you, that report keeps a copy of your content and, where applicable, the decision we took.

Technical data

Your device identifier for push notifications and the minimum data needed for your signed-in session.

Health data

Heart rate, the injuries you declare, your date of birth and your sex are health data, a specially protected category. We process them solely with your explicit consent, which you give when completing sign-up and when connecting Strava, and only for what section 3 says. You can withdraw it at any time by disconnecting Strava or deleting your account.

4. Artificial intelligence

Your coach is a language model that Ruki queries when you write to it. In order to answer you, that query sends your training data as context.

  • Your data is not used to train, fine-tune or evaluate any model.
  • No datasets are built from it.
  • The model provider does not train on information sent through its API.
  • Only your data enters your conversation: never another user's.

You are talking to a machine. The Ruki coach is an artificial intelligence system, not a person, and this is stated on the chat screen itself.

Automated decisions. Your plan is calculated by an algorithm from your data, and the coach can adjust it. These are training decisions: they produce no legal effects and do not similarly significantly affect you, and you can always ignore them, change them by hand or ask the coach to redo them. If you want a person to review it, write to us.

5. What other users can see

Other people using Ruki can see, from your profile: your name, your username, your photo, your city, your bio, your Instagram if you have added it, your current goal, your club and your number of friends.

They see nothing about your running: no kilometres, no paces, no heart rate, and no summary or chart calculated from them. Your activities, your plan, your conversations with the coach and the injuries you declare are private, and only you see them.

One point about the profile photo: it is stored at a public internet address, so anyone with that link can view it even if they do not use Ruki. That is normal in apps with photos, but it is worth knowing. When you delete your account, we delete the file too.

7. How long we keep it

For as long as your account is open. When you delete it, the following are deleted with you: profile, imported activities, plans, conversations, friendships and notifications.

Two exceptions, each for a specific reason:

  • Moderation reports are kept for up to one year from the date they are resolved, even if the account involved is deleted: this is what makes it possible to detect repeat offenders and to justify a decision if someone appeals it.
  • Anything a law requires us to keep, for the period that law sets.

8. How you delete your data

From the app itself, in Settings → Danger zone → Delete account. Deletion is immediate and irreversible. If you run a club, you are warned beforehand that the club will also disappear.

To stop importing activities without deleting your account: Settings → Connections → Disconnect Strava. We stop importing immediately; what has already been imported remains yours and you can remove it by deleting your account.

9. Your rights

By writing to manolojc02@gmail.com you can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability, and withdraw your consent at any time (withdrawing it does not affect what we did with it beforehand).

There is no "download my data" button in the app yet: ask by email and we will send you everything of yours in a readable file within one month at most.

If you believe we have not handled your request properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

10. Minors

Ruki is not aimed at anyone under 16 and we do not knowingly collect data from people under that age. If we detect an account belonging to someone under 16, we delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If something relevant changes, we will update this page and the date above, and we will let you know inside the app.
